# 🎧 Hear Learn

### Empowering Visually Impaired Students Through Accessible Education Technology

Hear Learn is a Flutter-based mobile application designed to bridge the educational gap for blind and partially sighted students. By combining accessibility-focused learning tools with practical daily-life assistance features, the application creates an inclusive learning environment where visual limitations are not a barrier to education.

---

## 🌟 Overview

Hear Learn connects visually impaired students and teachers through a shared digital learning platform. Educational content is transformed into accessible, voice-driven experiences using Text-to-Speech technology and intelligent recognition systems.

The project was developed after conducting interviews with visually impaired users to better understand their daily challenges and educational needs.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📚 Shared Learning Space

Teachers can upload learning materials directly to the platform.

**Key capabilities:**

* PDF document upload
* Automatic text extraction
* Text-to-Speech conversion
* Voice-based navigation
* Accessible learning experience

Students can listen to course content independently without requiring assistance from others.

---

### ✅ Voice-Enabled Quiz System

An assessment platform designed specifically for visually impaired learners.

**Features:**

* Teacher-created quizzes
* Voice-assisted questions
* Spoken answer choices
* Audio feedback
* Progress tracking

This allows students to complete assessments independently and confidently.

---

### 🎨 Color Recognition

A practical feature developed based on feedback from visually impaired users.

**How it works:**

* Point the camera at an object
* The app detects its color
* The detected color is announced through voice output

**Useful for:**

* Shopping for clothes
* Matching outfits
* Identifying household objects

---

### 💵 Currency Recognition

Promotes financial independence in everyday transactions.

**How it works:**

1. Take a picture of a banknote
2. The application analyzes the image
3. The denomination is announced through voice output

This helps users identify currency quickly and confidently.

---

### 🚨 Emergency Assistance

A one-touch emergency feature designed for safety.

**Capabilities:**

* Instant emergency alerts
* Location sharing
* Caregiver notification
* Quick access during urgent situations

Provides additional security and peace of mind for users and their families.
